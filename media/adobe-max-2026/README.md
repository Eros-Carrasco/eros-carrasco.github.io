# Adobe MAX 2026: photos and clips

Footage from the ASUS ProArt booth at Adobe MAX 2026 in Miami, where Eros Carrasco showed SplatLab live. SplatLab is his real-time engine for Gaussian splats. Visitors moved tables and cleared a scanned breakroom themselves, on two ASUS Ascent GX10s, with no cloud.

Project page: https://eros-carrasco.github.io/projects/splatlab/

All clips are 1080x1440 (portrait, 3:4), 30 fps, H.264, with no audio. No photo carries location data.

## Who is who

Eros wears a light blue plaid shirt, thin round metal glasses and a red Adobe lanyard. Everyone else in these files is a visitor. Do not name them.

## Notes from Eros for the video

- Do not use a shot from outside the booth.
- `demo-station-in-the-booth-scan.png` shows where his demo stood, inside his scan of the booth. Once the camera reaches that position, cut to the visitor using the app (`visitor-at-the-controls.mp4`).
- The people in the booth should be turned a little and look like they are working there, not standing like statues. Avoid showing them straight from the front, so their identity is not in the video.
